# Product Requirements

## Platform outcomes

SONARA must provide working, permission-aware customer software and operator-assisted services. Every visible capability must resolve to an honest state: working, ready, login required, workspace required, payment required, administrator setup required, database/storage/email/webhook/provider required, permission denied, temporarily unavailable, or explicitly in development.

## Shared platform kernel

- Authentication and persistent sessions
- Roles, organizations, workspaces, memberships, and tenant isolation
- Plans, entitlements, billing, requests, tasks, deliverables, files, notifications, support, audit, preferences, and feature flags
- Real status and reference IDs for writes
- Setup recovery paths instead of dead ends
- Human approval for high-risk AI or outbound actions

## Business Builder

- Produce useful business records and outputs: profiles, offers, customers, products/services, operations and launch checklists, orders/invoices, tasks, and configurable vertical templates.
- Preserve organization/workspace scope and paid/free module boundaries.

## Creator Studio

- Produce useful creator records and exportable artifacts: projects, assets, rights, releases, briefs, content calendars, production notes, tasks, and governed export packages.
- Never claim unsupported proprietary project formats or rights clearance.

## Growth Studio

- Produce campaign, lead, follow-up, content, experiment, analytics, and consent records.
- Opt-outs override outreach. Phone/SMS/voicemail execution remains disabled unless explicitly configured, consented, and reviewed.

## Launch quality

- No dead routes, fake buttons, fake metrics, simulated saves, or provider overclaims.
- Public, authenticated, product, and admin surfaces must be responsive and accessible.
- Paid access must be proven by database-backed entitlement state.
