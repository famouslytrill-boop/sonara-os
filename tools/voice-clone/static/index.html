Voice clone

Upload a voice, prove the speaker agreed, and type what they should say.

Checking what this can do…
1. Consent

A tickbox proves nothing. Get a phrase, have the speaker record themselves reading it aloud, and upload that recording. The phrase is different every time, so a clip recorded earlier cannot be used.

2. The voice

Ten to thirty seconds is plenty. Quiet room, no music.

3. What to say

The voice sample and the text do not have to be in the same language.
