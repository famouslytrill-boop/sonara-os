# DevOps Launch Monitoring Evaluation - June 2026

SONARA should begin with app-level health checks, Supabase checks, deployment checklists, rollback notes, and clear owner approvals. Prometheus-style metrics are acceptable behind a disabled adapter. Netdata is a monitoring reference. Docker/Moby-style containers are local-development documentation only.

Terraform/OpenTofu remain research and policy work until state handling, plan review, and rollback rules are ready. Ansible can be considered for simple runbooks later. Chef and Salt are deferred. Gradle is mobile/JVM research only. Production launch requires environment validation, checklist completion, rollback notes, and owner approval.
