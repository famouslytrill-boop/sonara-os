### Promotion rule

A screenshot with “inference available,” ZeroGPU, MCP, Agents, downloads, likes or trending status **does not** establish commercial rights, territory eligibility, model-weight license, dataset/output rights, privacy or retention behavior, safety/moderation behavior, GPU/latency/cost suitability, production availability, tenant isolation, or SONARA approval.

The Batch 23 model-hub promotion gate requires one exact resource/revision at a time to pass license, territory, serialization/custom-code, provenance, compute, safety, offline benchmark, provider/worker isolation and tenant-safe canary review before an explicit enable flag can exist.

Community variants labeled **uncensored, abliterated, NSFW, face swap, head swap, safety-removed or similar** are not capability targets. They remain blocked from runtime and are retained only as defensive provenance/refusal-integrity/consent research signals.
