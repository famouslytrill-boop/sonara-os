## License and safety findings

- AGPL/GPL projects such as Fluxer, ConvertX, gPTY, reNgine, TorBot, Fleetbase, and Recordly are not copied into SONARA. Their use requires a separate architecture and license decision.
- Mixed-license repositories such as GrowthBook, AFFiNE, and AutoGPT require path-level review. A repository badge is not sufficient.
- AlbertMicro and Awesome Social Engineering did not expose a verified license file during intake, so they remain reference-only.
- Hosted services and social graphics—including Higgsfield, SEO/shortcut graphics, PicoLM claims, RuView claims, Tel-Agent, training lists, and architecture diagrams—remain non-executable references until authoritative sources are verified.
- Security, OSINT, anti-bot, Tor, device-service, social-engineering, and Wi-Fi sensing leads cannot be used for unauthorized access, surveillance, evasion, identity tracing, or high-impact decisions.

## Claude, ChatGPT, and Codex strategy
