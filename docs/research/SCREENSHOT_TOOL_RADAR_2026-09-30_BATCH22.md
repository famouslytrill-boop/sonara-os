# Screenshot Tool Radar — 30 September 2026, Batch 22

## Decision

This intake represents the 30 screenshots uploaded in three groups. Five repository identities visible in the material were checked against their GitHub projects and primary project documentation: Microsoft Data Formulator, OpenH3-IR, Niko1221/Strata, the logo-design skill, and SirAllap/AgentGlass. Twenty-three visual and workflow references are cataloged without claiming they are production systems. Repeated, unattributed or cropped saved-reel leads remain visual leads until exact source and license can be established.

No screenshot binary, third-party artwork, code, agent skill, workflow, model, credentials, or customer data was copied. All five repository records are disabled and non-executing. This intake changes no billing, database schema, external connector, workflow activation, brand asset, or production authority.

## Visual patterns and boundaries

- **Web and commerce:** real-estate, ecommerce and logistics screens suggest clear search, categories, filters, offers, service detail, proof, tracking and contact routes. Their listings, prices, reviews, staff, locations, shipping metrics and trust claims are mock content.
- **Finance and administration:** dashboard charts, balances and payment history suggest visible source, currency, unit, time window, freshness and status. None of the displayed values are live or reconciled SONARA records.
- **Automation and agents:** n8n diagrams show webhook, code, decision, spreadsheet, calendar, email and social branches. A diagram proves neither credentials nor consent, tenant isolation, idempotency, retries, audit or safe side-effect approval. No workflow was imported or activated.
- **Developer and API education:** Git/GitHub/Vercel, server-role, API, Docker, package-manager and video-streaming infographics are learning aids, not source-of-truth runbooks. Commands and examples need current repository/framework validation; destructive and network effects must be explicit.
- **Media and local models:** video-generator lists, streaming designs, Strata, OpenH3-IR and creator dashboards raise model, weight, rights, storage, upload, transcode, bandwidth and cancellation questions. A mockup does not prove licensed output, streaming, DRM or a usable service.
- **Information architecture:** ecommerce and mobile task examples should map screen to registered route, actor and tenant authorization, record, durable status, error/retry/cancel behavior and accessibility checks.
- **Brand and product storytelling:** portfolio pages, logo grids, SONARA dashboard concept and the Muse article are inspiration only. Do not reuse third-party names, portraits, logos, project work, testimonials or claims. The Muse screenshot's identity/date/capabilities are not independently verified.
- **System-design and agent diagrams:** HackProduct grids for YouTube, Airbnb, Netflix, Uber, Instagram, agent routing, evaluations and production are educational sketches, not evidence of those companies' internal architectures or measured results.
- **Saved-reel grid:** cropped titles including Paperless-ngx, Remotion, browser-use and skill/tool roundups are leads only; the visible tiles do not consistently identify exact source, version, license or supported claims. They are not additional verified integrations.

## Product contracts added

The catalog turns the screenshots into six bounded questions: how research keeps source/license provenance; how each workflow step defines tenant scope, idempotency, retries, approval and compensation; how every metric identifies source and freshness; how responsive controls retain real routes and authorization; how media assets track rights, validation, storage and deletion; and how local agent tools remain isolated from customer tenants. These are review criteria, not newly enabled capabilities.

## Limits and next experiment

This is source-grounded repository review and screenshot analysis, not customer usability testing, trademark clearance, accessibility certification, model benchmarking or security approval. The next useful experiment is a synthetic-data walkthrough of one invoice/finance view and one media-upload path, verifying each visible action against an existing route, tenant record, source-of-truth state, accessible interaction, error recovery and deletion behavior. Keep every external candidate disabled until separately reviewed.
