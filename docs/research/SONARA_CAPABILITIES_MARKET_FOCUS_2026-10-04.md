# SONARA capabilities, market focus and advancement plan

As of 4 October 2026, America/New_York. Review competitor and upstream facts by 18 October 2026. Baseline inspected: `054ade9a38c5e3601c68061e9dfff29061c3d9b0`, merged PR #424. This report combines source inspection, current primary-source research and original implementation. It does not certify the production deployment, revenue, adoption or market leadership.

## Business and operating-system identity

SONARA Industries owns three product lines: Business Builder, Creator Studio and Growth Studio. SONARA One is their shared application platform. It is an application operating system: workspaces, records, permissions, workflows, commerce, media, formulas and governed integrations in a browser/server application. It does not replace Android, iOS, macOS, Windows or Linux; it has no independent kernel, hardware drivers or universal device privileges.

The inspected repository uses Express/Node and a Vercel deployment entry, Supabase/PostgreSQL identity and persistence, Stripe payment boundaries and Resend email configuration. Public pages, authenticated workspaces, organization membership, role controls, legal-template pages, payment webhook handling and setup-required states are present. The current architecture already has deterministic workflows and formulas, Creator Project Graph, merchant storefronts, rights-aware marketplace listings, public Growth channels and media/device components. The proposed competitive advantage is the continuity between doing work, producing usable media and measuring business outcomes.

## What SONARA can and cannot do

Business Builder can organize intake, customer records, appointments, jobs, operational checklists and configured payment pathways. Deterministic job states prevent invented transitions. Formula and financial modules support revenue, cost, payroll estimates, inventory and routing calculations. Storefront code is a foundation for connected commerce. Production providers and database deployment determine availability.

Creator Studio can manage original assets, versions, approvals, provenance, consent and release packages. Recent project code supports timeline edits, captions, local device drafts, local recording and WAV processing. Generation is an optional provider-backed pathway; outputs must carry provenance and remain subject to allowance, configuration, file access and rights rules. Marketplace listing is present; paid purchase and delivery remain unfinished.

Growth Studio can organize leads, offers, campaigns, experiments and recorded market evidence. Public channels provide an owned distribution surface. The source contains dispatch and attribution-related contracts, but no blanket claim that every social platform is connected is justified. Revenue attribution requires linked transactions and comparison designs; clicks alone do not prove incremental revenue.

SONARA cannot promise universal autonomous operation, unlimited generation, guaranteed profit, guaranteed search/social placement, market domination, arbitrary payment-terminal support, full offline synchronization or every external integration merely because research records exist. It is not a proven replacement for enterprise ERP/accounting, specialist CAD, a professional DAW, a game engine or a regulated clinical system. There is no verified market-share, retention or commercial scale result in this review.

## Recommended narrow market focus

Parent proposition: **Run the work, create the proof, grow repeat business.**

Initial hypothesis: owner-operated cleaning and property-service businesses with 1–10 people, first recruited locally in Columbus. This is a recommendation based on architectural fit and practical access, not established product-market fit. Cleaning offers recurring jobs, straightforward scheduling, visible work outcomes, repeat bookings and promotional-media needs without initially requiring deep restaurant hardware or enterprise trades infrastructure.

The connected workflow is consented inquiry → quote → scheduled job → completion → verified payment → approved media → approved follow-up → measured repeat booking. Business Builder owns the operation, Creator Studio packages approved evidence, and Growth Studio connects authorized follow-up to outcomes. Media consent must be independent of service acceptance. Customer locations and private interiors must not leak into public promotional material.

Narrow acquisition channels, default onboarding, templates and first-screen tasks. Preserve the underlying platform, all three product brands and broader vertical capabilities. Use industry packs over shared modules instead of separate forks. Keep advanced music, manufacturing, retail, transport and research workflows discoverable through contextual navigation, but do not make new service-business buyers configure them.

Creator Studio can additionally sell standalone to freelancers serving these businesses, then broaden to independent commercial creators. Growth Studio can serve the same operators and small agencies managing their accounts. Each product has a focused entry workflow while the shared platform remains broad.

Proposed 60-day test: interview 15 reachable operators; secure five genuinely paying pilots; seek four retained paying pilots at day 60. Record adoption, support time, completed jobs, payment reconciliation, export success and repeat booking. These thresholds are experiment choices, not existing results. If prospects do not pay or use the connected workflow repeatedly, revise the segment or offer before adding more modules.

## Deterministic expansion implemented in this branch

The existing formula-definition and evaluation routes now include six additional explicit evaluators. No dynamic `eval`, model call, new table or duplicate formula service is introduced.

| Key | Inputs and units | Computation | Example output | Meaning and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| expected_loss | Probability 0–1; loss in one currency | p × loss | 0.2 × 500 = 100 | Scenario expectation, not independently measured risk or tail-loss protection |
| smoothed_demand_level | Alpha 0–1; observation and prior level in the same units | alpha × observed + (1-alpha) × previous | 0.25 × 140 + 0.75 × 100 = 110 | Level smoothing; trend, seasonality and causal demand are not established |
| service_capacity_jobs | Available minutes; strictly positive minutes/job | floor(available / per-job) | 480 / 90 → 5 jobs | Assumes homogeneous jobs; does not solve travel or dispatch optimization |
| media_duration_seconds | Whole frame count; positive constant FPS | frames / FPS | 300 / 30 = 10 seconds | Constant-frame-rate timing; variable-frame-rate files require timestamps |
| media_payload_mebibytes | Bits/second; seconds | bitrate × duration / (8 × 1048576) | 8,388,608 × 60 → 60 MiB | Payload estimate excludes container, metadata, transcoding and duplicated storage |
| tracked_motion_speed | Calibrated displacement in meters; positive elapsed seconds | displacement / time | 12 / 3 = 4 m/s | Consumes measured displacement; does not detect poses or infer physical scale from pixels |

Inputs are bounded and invalid domains are refused. A boolean is not a measurement. Missing values are not zero. These numerical functions can be more reliable than a language model doing arithmetic because the execution rule is fixed and reproducible. They do not establish superiority to every AI system, and a precise formula still gives a bad decision when its measurements or assumptions are wrong.

The market-intelligence portfolio response also gains advisory focus assessments. Missing customer commitment, owner, segment, source, fresh timestamp or cost ceiling produces `validate`. Recorded same-period revenue and variable cost produce contribution and margin. Losses or exceeded ceilings block `pilot_candidate`. The assessment does not independently verify records or change roadmap state, campaigns, billing or deployment. It is decision support, not a new financial authority.

## Science, language, social research, CAD and motion roadmap

Use a common input/output contract: source, version, units, assumptions, valid domain, consent, uncertainty, result, reference fixture and reproducible execution. Distinguish exact arithmetic, heuristic scores, statistical estimates and learned perception. A deterministic downstream calculation does not make an upstream learned detector deterministic in accuracy.

Mathematics and computer science: expand with dimension checks, finite domain validation, graph scheduling, scenario comparison, rolling forecast evaluation and benchmark fixtures. Forecasts must beat a naive baseline on held-out periods before a customer claim of better prediction. Do not optimize against the same observations used for evaluation.

English and document workflows: template assembly, style constraints, document structure, terminology glossaries, captions and transcript editing can work without generative models. Meaning-sensitive proofreading, translation and summarization need human review and quality evaluation; rule-based checks cannot guarantee semantic correctness.

Social studies and market research: source-grounded timelines, demographic/public datasets, experiment records and uncertainty labels can support analysis. Segment scores remain hypotheses until interviews and paid behavior support them. Holdouts or phased experiments are needed before claiming incremental campaign lift.

Science and biology: unit-aware educational calculations and research records are possible extensions. Specific biology formulas, clinical recommendations or lab interpretation require separately validated domain definitions. No biology/clinical capability was implemented in this branch, and scientific legitimacy cannot be obtained by attaching the word biology to a general formula engine.

CAD: begin with project-linked file references, revision history and portable STEP/DXF/SVG handoffs to specialist tools. AutoCAD is a proprietary product, so an integration needs its authorized API/service and licensing. No CAD kernel, native DWG round-trip fidelity or manufacturing-grade geometry is claimed. FreeCAD and Blender are research candidates needing current source, license and artifact compatibility review.

Motion: evaluate MediaPipe Pose Landmarker for optional landmark extraction. Its official documentation explicitly describes an ML model with image/world-coordinate outputs. Keep raw video opt-in, store only required derived data, handle missing/confidence-low points and evaluate scale/occlusion. The new motion-speed formula accepts calibrated measurements; it is not a motion-capture engine.

Rendering: use Creator Project Graph as an edit specification; pre-render low-resolution proxies, estimate payload and resource costs, then render final outputs in bounded workers. Require cancellation, timeout, private job storage, retries and deterministic manifest/version recording. Browser hardware support must be detected; a web application cannot promise any graphics card or sustained mobile GPU budget.

## Free/open-source adoption decisions

The locked existing application dependencies were installed for verification. These include OpenFeature and OpenTelemetry packages. No bulk third-party model, CAD package, media framework or new infrastructure runtime was installed. Current upstream license files were checked for the candidates below, but version pinning, advisories, actual model rights and workload benchmarks remain adoption requirements.

| Candidate | Current verified upstream/code license | Best placement | Required proof before runtime enablement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| whisper.cpp | ggml-org/whisper.cpp; MIT | Isolated transcription worker | Model-weight license, pinned release, word error rate, memory/time bounds and licensed audio |
| ONNX Runtime | microsoft/onnxruntime; MIT | Optional inference adapter | Actual model compatibility and license; device/GPU fallback; quality/latency benchmark |
| Transformers.js | huggingface/transformers.js; Apache-2.0 | Optional browser/local inference experiment | Model size/license, download UX, privacy boundaries and supported device proof |
| FFmpeg | FFmpeg/FFmpeg; LGPL baseline with configuration-dependent GPL/nonfree components | Isolated media worker | Exact binary/build license, codec choices, resource limits and malformed-file handling |
| LiveKit | livekit/livekit; Apache-2.0 | Optional realtime media service | Real network/TURN tests, consent/retention, bandwidth cost and authorization |

Hugging Face hosts model artifacts and model cards. Repository code license, model weights, datasets and service terms are separate. Public download does not mean unrestricted commercial use. Keep local inference optional and preserve deterministic workflows when no model is configured. Do not substitute more runtimes for SONARA's existing Provider Gateway or pnpm authority.

## Delivery sequence and economics

First: prove login, paid access and the single quote/job/payment loop on the actual deployment. Second: approved work-media export and consented repeat-booking attribution. Third: marketplace checkout/private delivery. Fourth: one real external connector with recovery. Fifth: benchmark heavy-media and optional local-model workers. Broader CAD, biology and gaming/streaming infrastructure stay staged until a paying use case justifies their complexity.

Use existing canonical records and migrations. Add SQL only for a demonstrated missing invariant. Marketplace delivery needs explicit payment/order/entitlement authority; its migration design must be checked against existing commerce before introducing tables. Deployment follows exact-head CI, migration replay, controlled rollout, two-tenant/customer checks and rollback evidence.

Contribution = subscription revenue − variable provider, storage, egress, payment and support cost. Contribution margin = contribution / revenue. At an illustrative $59 revenue and $12 variable cost, contribution is $47 and margin is approximately 79.66%; this is an arithmetic example, not SONARA's measured margin. Fixed hosting, development, tax and founder compensation are additional expenses. Free marketplace access with zero SONARA commission does not create transaction revenue for the parent. Profitability needs measured retained paid use and cost control, not speculative market-size multiplication.

## Implementation boundaries

This branch changes decision support, formula execution and source documentation. It does not complete the whole expansion roadmap. It applies no production SQL, takes no customer payment, sends no customer campaign, enables no third-party runtime and certifies no production deployment. Broader market dominance, professional CAD/motion capture, clinical biology and universal autonomous operation are not delivered or promised.
