# Business OS Benchmark Evaluation - June 2026

Odoo is a useful benchmark for broad business operating systems, but SONARA must not clone Odoo code or present itself as an ERP clone. The useful lesson is modular coverage: CRM, quotes, bookings, payments, reviews, records, website surfaces, inventory records, and project pipelines all need shared customer records and permissions.

Business Builder should own sub-apps, records, bookings, quotes, and practical money-path workflows. Creator Studio should own creator assets, release readiness, rights review, and media metadata. Growth Studio should own campaigns, offers, review requests, referrals, and customer follow-up planning. Shared SONARA infrastructure should provide auth, RBAC, audit logs, search, support, provider registry, and launch gates.

Modules must work independently, share organization-scoped customer records, and avoid accounting, payroll, tax, legal, compliance, or outcome guarantees.
