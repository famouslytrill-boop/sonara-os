# SONARA Industries — contract and agreement pack for qualified legal review

**Internal drafting workbook only. DO NOT publish, sign, auto-send, or represent as legally binding.** No attorney review has occurred. Every placeholder must be resolved, every applicable rule verified, every executed version stored unchanged, and approval recorded by licensed counsel for the jurisdiction and specific use case. The software provider, property owner, seller, merchant, and buyer may have different legal duties. The contracting entity is intentionally blank; a product brand is not automatically a legal entity.

This workbook is designed for legal and product teams to produce **separate** agreements: A. SONARA customer financial-services disclosures, B. optional seller/merchant payment addendum, C. landlord-tenant lease schedule (property owner's agreement), D. equipment rental/lease schedule (equipment owner's agreement), E. digital marketplace license grant. No clause below replaces state/federal statutory consumer rights.

**1. Products and capabilities.** Identify plan purchased ([Business Builder / Creator Studio / Growth Studio / combined]), actual features and entitlements, execution limits, supported regions and currently unavailable or conditional services. **2. Customer sees required charges before agreement. No surprise renewal, undisclosed fee, backdated auto-charge or unexplained payment redirection.

**3. Cancellation and refunds.** [Customer-controlled cancellation location or alternate accessible channel], [effective cancellation date], [billing after cancellation], [retained access/records], [export and deletion deadline subject to lawful holds], [refund eligibility], [support/dispute route], [jurisdictional overrides]. Counsel confirms compatibility with actual Stripe products and federal/state law. Platform must not claim automatic refunds that it cannot initiate.

**4. Payments and processor boundary.** Subscription fees are charged by [Stripe or actual disclosed provider] on [verified SONARA platform merchant]. SONARA must disclose processor involvement, card-handling limits and reasonable escalation for unauthorized payment reports. Never solicit card PIN/CVV in SONARA forms. If a paid transaction is missing, an error in a processor notification must not be treated as proof of nonpayment.

**5. Security, data and interruptions.** [privacy policy and DPA URL/version], [subprocessors], [data retention, export and erasure], [incident notice], [service availability commitments reviewed by counsel], [support access and dispute escalation], [breach remedy], [accessibility]. Security capabilities are commitments only where deployed and verified, not because an architecture diagram says so.

**6. Electronic consent.** [paper option and withdrawal terms when required], [software/hardware access statement], [consent timestamp and method], [able-to-retain demonstration], [versioned record receipt]. Retain immutable document version and delivery evidence; do not treat a prechecked box or inferred visit as valid consent.

**7. Limitations and dispute resolution.** Counsel determines permissible scope of warranties, liability, indemnity, dispute venue, choice of law, statutory nonwaivable rights, required arbitration/opt-outs if chosen, and remedies. Neither side is required to forfeit nonwaivable rights.

## B. SONARA payment-enabled seller/merchant — connected account addendum

**Parties:** [SONARA legal entity], [seller legal entity], [Stripe connected account governed by provider contract]. Seller authority checked by [verification process]. Money beneficiary: [seller organization], not a buyer-input field.

**1. Seller-of-record representation.** Seller identifies its own product/service, applicable taxes, fulfillment, consumer refunds and customer care; legal/tax counsel verifies whether seller actually acts as merchant of record. SONARA supplies software and approved provider integration, not bank, custodian, issuer, money transmitter, escrow provider or lawyer, unless SONARA separately obtains appropriate authority.

**2. Routing and funds.** Intended mode: Stripe Connect direct charges on seller's verified connected account, with no platform marketplace commission in current Creator model. Actual cost, fees, balances, reserves, losses, disputes, payout timing and processor access depend on real Stripe contract and account configuration. **SONARA may still face connected-account negative balance and reserve exposure even on direct charges.** A payment success page is not settlement proof. No change of payout destination via email or a user-submitted URL.

**3. Fraud / disputes.** Seller consents to verification of merchant identity/ownership, charge/refund matching, receipt audit, fraud escalation and lawful suspension where specific controls authorize it. Reported fraud triggers proportionate review; it is not proof of wrongdoing. Reserve/hold and payout rules must be based on provider agreement and lawful notice, never arbitrary withholding by SONARA.

**4. Returns and chargebacks.** Detail refund policy, disclosure timing, chargeback evidence, response deadlines, authorized merchant action, provider's decision and adjustment entries. SONARA cannot promise chargeback reversal or guarantee seller funds. Partial and repeated refunds need original-charge linkage and cents-accurate ceilings.

**5. Customer communications.** Only verified in-app checkout routes or provider-hosted payment links; forwarded invoices and unsolicited messages never become proof of beneficiary change. Legitimate contact change requires step-up and independent customer/owner verification via existing trusted channel.

**6. Prohibited uses.** No illegal sales, unlicensed financial activity, sanctions violations, laundering, fraudulent invoices, fake proof/reviews, identity theft, deceptive media, counterfeit goods, or IP infringement. Counsel controls the enforcement process, appeal, data retention and mandatory reporting.

**7. Data and closure.** Termination, remaining balances, outstanding disputes, customer records, refunds, statutory records, seller export and external provider account status are separate. Disconnecting an integration never means destroying provider transaction history.

## F. Evidence checklist for every executed agreement

For each transaction the real product must separately preserve:
- exact final text digest and version + counsel scope (type/jurisdiction/date), not just a mutable `approved` Boolean;
- authenticated human parties and signer authority, acceptance method, affirmative consent/paper options, actual delivery receipt, immutable readable copy;
- money quote version; mandatory fee/deposit/tax split; correct beneficiary and merchant-of-record; provider verification reference;
- renew/cancel/return/repair/notice deadlines, dispute/appeal route, legal hold and version history;
- testable RLS and organization ownership; records protected against silent overwrites;
- no generated terms automatically published, signed, charged or enforced by AI or automation.

**Counsel signoff fields:** reviewer name/bar and engagement [ ], states/transactions [ ], reviewed final document digests [ ], jurisdiction/effective scope [ ], known exceptions [ ], expires/review trigger [ ], owner acceptance [ ], QA evidence [ ], public URL/version if separately approved [ ].
