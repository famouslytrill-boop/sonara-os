# SONARA Creator Studio Product Roadmap

Creator Studio is the SONARA Industries product surface for creation, optimization, release, and catalog scaling. This roadmap keeps product work aligned to Create, Optimize, Release, and Scale.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the Create to Export flow: Signal Initialization, Analyze Intelligence, Compose System, Mutation Lab, Export Forge.
- Keep visible copy professional, cinematic, detached, and modern.
- Keep Explorer, Architect, and Label as internal operating modes only.
- Preserve Rights Passport, provenance, and anti-clone safety in every export path.
- Keep final export tiers limited to `prompt_bundle`, `production_bundle`, `daw_bundle`, `release_bundle`, and `elite_mutation_bundle`.

## Roadmap 1: MVP Completion

1. Stabilize Signal Initialization upload simulation and session persistence.
2. Keep Analyze Intelligence deterministic until real analysis adapters are configured.
3. Keep Compose System local and typed around composer sheet plus master prompt.
4. Keep Mutation Lab variants scored with Critic Engine and Market Radar language.
5. Keep Export Forge downloads local, provenance-aware, and tier-safe.

## Roadmap 2: Launch Readiness

6. Confirm all required launch routes remain registered and smoke-covered.
7. Keep Launch Readiness focused on route health, media readiness, export tiers, safety, and provenance.
8. Keep Account auth-ready without blocking local MVP usage.
9. Keep Download Center local until storage is configured.
10. Keep media and sound readiness optional and user-activated.

## Roadmap 3: Release Operating Layer

11. Harden Release Engine scheduling states.
12. Keep content planning aligned to release lift and campaign precision.
13. Keep Visualizer Studio scaffolded until media generation adapters are configured.
14. Keep campaign surfaces local and reversible.
15. Add tests for release-state bounds and empty states.

## Roadmap 4: Catalog Intelligence

16. Normalize Catalog Intelligence scoring and labels.
17. Keep licensing and revenue signals as local decision scaffolds.
18. Keep Creator CRM and submission surfaces typed and local.
19. Add Rights Passport status into catalog-facing export summaries.
20. Add smoke coverage for catalog state integrity.

## Roadmap 5: Signature Engine

21. Add typed signature profile scaffolding.
22. Keep signature memory local until durable storage is configured.
23. Connect signature profiles to master prompt generation only through typed adapters.
24. Add prompt lineage to Rights Passport payloads.
25. Add tests for signature profile serialization.

## Roadmap 6: Signal Conductor

26. Define a subsystem registry for Signal Initialization, Analyze Intelligence, Compose System, Mutation Lab, Export Forge, Release Engine, Catalog Intelligence, and Rights Passport.
27. Keep orchestration visual-only until provider and storage adapters are configured.
28. Route all AI-facing tasks through Provider Gateway.
29. Attach safety warnings and provenance notes to conductor decisions.
30. Add Launch Readiness checks for conductor registry status.

## Completion Gate

Before any product expansion, run:

```sh
pnpm run typecheck
pnpm run build
pnpm run smoke
```

Continue only when the app remains stable, route-safe, export-tier-safe, and free of blocked visible wording.
