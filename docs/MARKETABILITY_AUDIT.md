## Current Positioning

SONARA Industries is positioned as a technology holding company that owns independent software companies. SONARA Industries™ is positioned as the shared launch platform for:

- Business Builder: build trust, collect setup details, prepare booking/payment/review workflows.
- Creator Studio: package creative services, organize assets, track rights notes, prepare client-ready proof.
- Growth Studio: plan campaigns, reviews, referrals, and follow-up without fake metrics.

This structure is marketable because it avoids a generic “all-in-one” claim and gives each audience a distinct reason to care.

## CTA And Funnel Review

Current funnel:

1. `/` explains SONARA Industries™ and links to `/onboarding` and `/pricing`.
2. `/business-builder`, `/creator-studio`, and `/growth-studio` explain the product surfaces.
3. `/pricing` explains plan ranges and setup services.
4. `/contact` routes setup, support, product access, and security questions.
5. `/onboarding` lets users choose a product path and save local setup progress.

This is clear enough for beta. Paid launch needs deployed checkout, support routing, and real account setup.

## Marketing Safety

Rules currently satisfied:

- No guaranteed income claims.
- No guaranteed customer claims.
- No guaranteed growth claims.
- No fake testimonials.
- No fake reviews.
- No fake customer logos.
- No deceptive urgency.
- No hidden-fee language.
- No legal, tax, financial, or security advice claims.

Rules to keep enforcing:

- Do not call beta/admin systems live.
- Do not imply AI or automation can execute risky work without owner approval.
- Do not imply Stripe payouts, refunds, or disputes are controlled by SONARA.
- Do not publish legal/policy text without owner/legal review.

## Marketability Verdict

Status: `needs_review`.

The product story is understandable and launchable for a controlled beta. Public paid launch should remain blocked until Stripe, auth, production domain, legal pages, and end-to-end onboarding/billing verification are complete.
