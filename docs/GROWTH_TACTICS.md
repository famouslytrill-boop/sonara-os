# Growth Tactics

Growth Studio now has a tactic and checklist foundation.

Implemented:
- Public Growth Studio tactics page.
- Dashboard Growth tactics page.
- Admin Growth tactics review page.
- Tactic categories for onboarding, notification permission, paywall, reviews, referrals, landing pages, email, retention, social proof, and competitor research notes.
- Checklist templates for mobile launch, web launch, landing pages, app store optimization, onboarding, review requests, notification permission, paywall tests, social proof, and competitor ad research.

Safety rules:
- No fake analytics.
- No fake reviews.
- No spam automation.
- No guaranteed revenue claims.
- Tactics are drafts until implemented and measured.
