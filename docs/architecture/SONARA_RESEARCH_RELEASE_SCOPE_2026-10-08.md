# SONARA engineering scope — October 8, 2026

SONARA Industries is the parent, SONARA One is the shared application platform, and Business Builder, Creator Studio and Growth Studio are its three product operations. This is an engineering plan, not a production capability claim.

Focus: role-scoped customer organization administration, free social/storefront/marketplace access, private tenant boundaries, rights-cleared discovery, Android/iOS operations, CSV/Excel integration, media jobs, consent, accessibility and release governance.

Keep production blocked until exact-head release checks are green. Do not apply production migrations or publish binding legal policies from this document.
