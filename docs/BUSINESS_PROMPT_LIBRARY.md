# Business Prompt Library

Business Builder prompt templates help owners draft blueprints, landing page copy, value offers, launch plans, MVP validation plans, customer responses, website copy improvements, job descriptions, emails, and tool comparisons.

Rules:

- Use only real business facts supplied by the user.
- Do not invent proof, outcomes, credentials, reviews, or customer claims.
- Keep customer-facing drafts in owner review before use.
- Do not present output as legal, tax, financial, or professional advice.
