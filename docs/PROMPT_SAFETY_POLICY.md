# Prompt Safety Policy

Prompt templates must be original, structured, and draft-only.

Blocked prompt requests include:

- final-authority legal, tax, financial, or investment advice
- fake reviews or testimonials
- deceptive marketing
- fake scarcity or urgency
- impersonation
- scraping private or protected sites
- spam outreach
- customer manipulation
- sensitive personal data collection
- platform rule bypassing
- credential handling
- publishing generated output without owner approval

Owner approval is required for customer-facing campaigns, legal or policy copy, pricing copy, refund or dispute messaging, review or testimonial publishing, public ads, cold outreach, and AI voice, visual, or video prompts intended for public output.
