# SONARA Market Research and Development Action Plan — 2026

## Executive decision

SONARA should not respond to market growth by cloning mature competitors. The company should reduce setup friction, prove value faster, connect records across the three studios, preserve customer ownership, and measure economic outcomes before expanding scope.

The market-intelligence system remains the evidence store. This action plan adds the validation portfolio that determines what should enter Product Lifecycle.

## Current market conclusions

### Small-business operating software

Small businesses and nonemployer operators form a large addressable market, but digital adoption remains uneven. Current research emphasizes adoption of practical cloud, CRM, and operations tools. Small teams increasingly prefer simple outcomes, interoperability, and reduced tool fragmentation.

**SONARA implication:** Business Builder should lead with the first completed transaction, guided setup, portable records, and vertical starter packs—not an unrestricted blank builder or full accounting replacement.

### Creator operations

Creators increasingly identify as business owners and need payment, business, ownership, and monetization infrastructure. Creator advertising is a major channel, while creator selection, measurement, standards, provenance, and business-outcome reporting remain incomplete.

**SONARA implication:** Creator Studio should lead with rights-aware release packages, portable exports, direct audience ownership, collaboration evidence, and brand-deal operations—not generic generation volume.

### CRM, lifecycle marketing, and measurement

Marketing teams are simplifying technology stacks and prioritizing usability, reporting, attribution, integration, and ROI evidence. Privacy constraints increase the value of first-party data, consent, offline conversions, controlled experiments, and confidence labels.

**SONARA implication:** Growth Studio should occupy the governed middle between a basic sender and an enterprise marketing suite. It should not depend on autonomous sending, publishing, ad mutation, or last-click certainty.

## Cross-company fixes

1. **Time to first value** — instrument the first-value event, elapsed setup time, abandonment, and assisted setup for every studio.
2. **Shared evidence spine** — use one customer, consent, suppression, source, asset, transaction, and attribution timeline.
3. **Value and cost scorecards** — measure gross margin, provider cost, support minutes, activation, retained usage, and workflow completion.
4. **Portability** — make imports, exports, provenance, approvals, and audit evidence first-class capabilities.
5. **Validation before roadmap** — require a reachable segment, measurable commitment, owner, cost ceiling, success threshold, and kill criterion.

## 90-day validation portfolio

### Business Builder

- Run a 10-owner concierge first-transaction pilot, but measure the full lead-to-cash path rather than only setup completion.
- Validate vertical packs against the same shared primitives: restaurant/food, trades and field service, retail/ecommerce, fleet/logistics, professional services, cleaning/facilities, property/rental, events/hire, memberships, and light manufacturing.
- Complete a real customer/service/catalog import trial plus export/re-import proof so portability is measurable rather than promised.
- Instrument quote-to-job, job-to-invoice, order-to-payment, payment-to-reconciliation, inventory-to-reorder, and schedule-to-time loops.
- Keep models above deterministic business commands: AI may classify, extract, summarize, draft, rank, plan, and propose next steps; authorization, entitlements, money math, inventory/job/invoice/payment state, refunds, and audit writes remain deterministic.
- Require approval for outbound customer messages, refunds, payouts, destructive changes, publication, high-impact permission changes, and external purchases.
- Build RAG only over organization-scoped records and explicitly approved documents, preserving ACLs, provenance, citations, freshness, retrieval traces, and evaluation.
- Measure first transaction activation, setup time, repeat use, support minutes, gross margin, provider cost, workflow completion, exception rate, retry/dead-letter rate, and manual-approval rate.

### Creator Studio

- Run a 10-creator portable release-package pilot.
- Track five representative brand partnerships from brief through approval, disclosure, and measurement handoff.
- Test consented fan/buyer record ownership and export.
- Measure project completion, export success, rights completeness, and repeat project creation.

### Growth Studio

- Reconcile one end-to-end customer timeline for five organizations.
- Design five holdout or phased incrementality tests with explicit hypotheses and stop conditions.
- Measure five creator partnerships using source, confidence, freshness, disclosure, and attribution limitations.
- Track consent completeness, deduplication, offline conversion matching, experiment completion, and evidence freshness.

## Pricing and packaging

The canonical runtime ladder is Free $0, One workspace $29/month, All three $59/month, and Team $109/month, with annual twins priced at ten months of the corresponding monthly plan when configured. Business Builder setup is quoted rather than assigned a silent fixed checkout price. Keep market strategy synchronized with that runtime catalog. Cost-bearing model/tool execution, email, SMS, storage, generation, provider API usage, and high-touch support require measurable included usage, customer-supplied accounts, or separate credits/usage pricing.
