# SONARA Market Intelligence — 2026

## Purpose

SONARA Industries uses one evidence-led market intelligence system across Business Builder, Creator Studio, and Growth Studio. The system records customer segments, market signals, competitor evidence, pricing, scored opportunities, review decisions, and links to Product Lifecycle initiatives.

Market size alone does not authorize development. An opportunity must show reachable customers, a costly or urgent problem, willingness to pay or another credible commitment, strategic fit, differentiation, and a feasible delivery path.

## Portfolio position

SONARA should not attempt to become another generic website builder, design editor, accounting package, bulk-email platform, or unrestricted generation service.

The stronger position is an affordable governed operating layer that connects:

- a founder's first offer, intake, booking or order, payment, delivery, and repeat customer;
- a creator's assets, rights evidence, collaborators, release package, direct audience, and monetization records;
- a company's first-party customer data, purpose-specific consent, touchpoints, offline conversions, experiments, attribution evidence, and measured learning;
- market evidence to Product Lifecycle decisions from discovery through launch and scale.

The shared moat is continuity and evidence: one organization, customer, consent, asset, source, decision, and audit spine across the three studios.

## Market 1 — Business Builder

### Market conditions

The United States contains more than 36 million small businesses. Nonemployer businesses account for the majority of establishments, which means a large part of the market consists of owners without dedicated operations, finance, marketing, or technical teams.

Small businesses are investing in cybersecurity, social media, content, digital operations, and selected AI-assisted workflows, but adoption remains uneven. Small firms frequently need simple setup and reliable workflows more than an expansive feature catalog.

Mature competitors already bundle payments, point of sale, websites, bookings, invoicing, customer records, inventory, and staff tools. Their advanced plans frequently cost much more than SONARA's current subscription ladder. Competing feature-for-feature would create high delivery cost and weak differentiation.

### Business Builder priority

Business Builder should own the guided path to the first completed transaction:

1. Define an offer or service.
2. Publish or share an intake path.
3. Capture a booking, request, order, or lead.
4. Collect payment through an authorized provider.
5. Create the customer and fulfillment record.
6. Deliver the work.
7. Request a review or referral with consent.
8. Follow up for repeat business.
9. Review cash-flow and operational evidence without making accounting or tax claims.

### Recommended additions

- Vertical starter packs for service businesses, restaurants and food operators, mobile vendors, consultants, and creator-led services.
- Import and export workflows that reduce switching friction.
- Business security readiness for staff permissions, payment boundaries, recovery, backups, and account access.
- First-transaction activation metrics and time-to-value reporting.
- Cash-flow and operating-health snapshots based on posted records, clearly separated from licensed accounting advice.
- Customer ownership and portable records as an explicit product principle.

### Do not build yet

- A full accounting, tax filing, banking, or payroll replacement.
- Unlimited concierge support inside low-price subscriptions.
- A complete feature clone of Square, Shopify, Wix, QuickBooks, or HoneyBook.
- Autonomous financial decisions or unsupported profitability guarantees.

## Market 2 — Creator Studio

### Market conditions

Creator advertising is now a substantial media channel. Buyers increasingly treat creators as a core channel, but the market still has fragmented measurement, inconsistent standards, creator-selection risk, audience-authenticity questions, and weak business-outcome reporting.

Creators want direct fan relationships, sustainable businesses, and stronger control over their work and audience. General-purpose creation is dominated by large design and media suites. Membership platforms may charge percentage-based platform fees in addition to payment processing.

SONARA's defensible opportunity is not generic asset generation. It is creator operations, ownership, provenance, portable releases, collaboration, direct-audience records, and evidence connecting creative work to business outcomes.

### Creator Studio priority

Creator Studio should own the path from original work to a portable, rights-aware release or client package:

1. Establish creator or artist identity and project intent.
2. Record source references, rights, consent, provenance, and originality checks.
3. Manage assets, tracks, stems, versions, collaborators, and approvals.
4. Generate or import optional provider output with provider metadata retained.
5. Prepare release, client, distribution, or archive packages.
6. Capture direct buyers, fans, collaborators, and brand-partner records with consent.
7. Hand campaign and conversion evidence to Growth Studio.
8. Measure repeated project completion, successful export, direct revenue, and retention.

### Recommended additions

- Rights, consent, provenance, source-reference, and collaborator-role records attached to each release package.
- Portable DAW, distributor, client, brand-partner, and archive exports.
- Creator-owned fan and buyer records with consent and suppression controls.
- Brand partnership briefs, deliverables, approvals, FTC disclosure readiness, and measurement handoff.
- Human-controlled generation, with provider readiness and setup requirements clearly shown.
- Version, approval, and provenance history for generated and imported media.

### Do not build yet

- Template-volume competition against Canva or Adobe.
- Unrestricted identity imitation or voice cloning.
- Guaranteed streams, audience growth, sponsorships, distribution acceptance, or copyright clearance.
- Provider-dependent workflows that fail when an external account is absent.

## Market 3 — Growth Studio

### Market conditions

Digital advertising and creator advertising continue to grow, but customer journeys are fragmented across channels and devices. Reliable measurement increasingly depends on first-party data, consented signals, offline conversions, deduplication, cross-channel reporting, and controlled experiments.

Basic CRM and email tools can be inexpensive or free. Advanced automation, attribution, governance, onboarding, and data operations become significantly more expensive. SONARA should occupy the gap between a basic sender and an enterprise marketing suite.

### Growth Studio priority

Growth Studio should own governed first-party growth operations:

1. Define the target segment and customer problem.
2. Record consent by purpose and channel.
3. Plan campaign, creator partnership, or experiment hypotheses.
4. Approve content and provider operations.
5. Record deduplicated touchpoints and offline conversions.
6. Store attribution model, confidence, sampling, and freshness evidence.
7. Separate correlation from verified incremental lift.
8. Connect leads and customers to Business Builder transactions and Creator Studio assets.
9. Review activation, retention, churn, revenue retention, and support impact.

### Recommended additions

- One consent ledger and customer timeline across the three studios.
- Server-controlled first-party data and offline-conversion workflows.
- Incrementality and holdout experiment planning.
- Creator partnership measurement and disclosure checks.
- Answer-engine visibility tracking based on observable citations and referrals, without guaranteed-placement claims.
- Provider diagnostics, suppression handling, and human approval before messages, publishing, ads, or budget changes.

### Do not build yet

- High-volume email or SMS sending without provider approval, deliverability controls, suppression, consent, and monitoring.
- Autonomous advertising or budget changes.
- Last-click attribution presented as certainty.
- Modeled, sampled, or stale results without clear labels.

## Pricing and packaging

Current owner-approved plans:

- Free — $0
- Starter — $7 per month
- Core — $19 per month
- Pro — $39 per month
- Business Builder — optional one-time setup purchase

This ladder is materially below many mature competitors. The price advantage should be preserved, but it cannot imply unlimited costly usage.

### Packaging policy

- **Free:** one organization, core records, readiness checks, limited active work, manual exports.
- **Starter:** one studio's essential workflow with practical record and storage limits.
- **Core:** multi-studio connections, customer timeline, richer exports, lifecycle evidence, and team collaboration.
- **Pro:** experiments, attribution evidence, portfolio intelligence, higher limits, and priority support while retaining safety gates.
- **Usage-bearing operations:** email, SMS, storage, media generation, and third-party APIs require documented quotas, customer-supplied provider accounts, or separately priced usage.

Do not change public prices solely because a competitor charges more. Price changes require customer evidence, cost-to-serve data, conversion impact, retention evidence, and owner approval.

## Opportunity scoring

Each opportunity receives a transparent score:

### Positive evidence

- Demand evidence: 0–25
- Willingness to pay: 0–20
- Strategic fit: 0–20
- Underserved need: 0–15
- Differentiation: 0–10
- Reachable channel access: 0–10

### Risk penalties

- Delivery complexity: 0–15
- Compliance risk: 0–15

### Decision bands

- 75–100: prioritize
- 55–74: validate
- 35–54: watch
- 0–34: hold

A score does not automatically authorize development. A review must record rationale and evidence. A prioritized opportunity should be linked to a Product Lifecycle initiative.

## Evidence contract

Every market signal must record:

- studio or parent company;
- signal type;
- title and summary;
- source publisher and HTTPS URL;
- observed date;
- geography and segment when relevant;
- confidence level;
- expiry date for time-sensitive evidence;
- metric value and unit when applicable.

Facts, customer quotes, estimates, assumptions, and internal opinions must remain distinguishable. Competitor pricing must carry a verified date. The platform must never populate an organization's private market workspace with fictional customers, competitors, interviews, or market results.

## Operating cadence

### Weekly

- Review new customer interviews, support themes, competitor changes, and market signals.
- Mark expired evidence.
- Re-score opportunities only when evidence changes.
- Confirm the next validation action and owner.

### Monthly

- Review the opportunity portfolio by studio.
- Compare time-to-value, activation, retention, churn, support load, gross margin, and provider cost.
- Decide prioritize, validate, watch, hold, or reject.
- Link approved opportunities to Product Lifecycle.

### Quarterly

- Revalidate target segments and pricing assumptions.
- Review competitive movement and switching friction.
- Retire initiatives with weak evidence or unfavorable economics.
- Confirm SONARA remains affordable without concealing cost-bearing limits.
