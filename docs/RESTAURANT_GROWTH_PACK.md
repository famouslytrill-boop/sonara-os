# Restaurant Growth Pack

Restaurant modules are future-flagged and disabled by default.

Feature flags:
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_RESTAURANT_PACK=false`
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_RESTAURANT_AI_RECEPTIONIST=false`

Planned scope:
- Missed call tracking placeholder.
- Reservation inquiry script builder.
- Event and catering inquiry scripts.
- Guest FAQ scripts.
- Hours, location, and menu question scripts.
- Human handoff checklist.
- Data ownership messaging.

Not shipped before launch:
- Live phone answering.
- Outbound calling.
- Ringless voicemail.
- Full reservation platform.
- Voice provider dependency.
- Fake 24/7 answering claims.
