# Legacy Cleanup Report

Date: 2026-06-04

## Public Branding Policy

Public launch pages must use SONARA Industries, Business Builder, Creator Studio, or Growth Studio. Old product names are not active public products.

## Compatibility Redirects

Old public URLs are kept only as redirects for backlink compatibility. They resolve to current product or trust routes and are not presented in navigation, metadata, sitemap, or product copy.

## Allowed Historical Locations

Historical references are allowed only in:

- `docs/audits/*`
- `docs/archive/*`
- historical migration comments when unavoidable

## Current Repair

- Added route checks that require legacy URLs to remain redirect-only.
- Added public route and sitemap checks that require modern launch routes.
- Preserved compatibility redirects without adding old names to sitemap or public navigation.
