# Launch Offer Strategy

Date: 2026-05-21

Status: needs owner review before public paid launch.

The launch offer should be simple: help customers build a credible profile, prove trust, get paid through approved provider links, and prepare growth follow-up without fake promises or unsafe automation.

## Core Offer

Build. Prove. Get paid. Grow.

SONARA Industries™ should be sold as a practical launch system for small businesses, creators, and growth operators. The first offer should focus on setup clarity, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Founding Member Offer

Purpose: reward early users without deceptive urgency.

Safe wording:

- Founding Member pricing is available for an initial beta cohort.
- Spaces or dates should only be used if the limit is real.
- Pricing may change later, but existing billing terms should be honored as documented.
- No guaranteed revenue, customer, growth, or ranking outcomes.

Do not use fake countdowns, fake scarcity, fake testimonials, or fake customer logos.

## Done-For-You Launch Package

Recommended bundle:

- Business profile setup.
- Payment option review using provider-hosted links.
- Booking link or booking request setup.
- Intake questions.
- Review request link setup.
- Trust/safety checklist.
- Launch checklist handoff.

Boundaries:

- No raw card data.
- No CVV storage.
- No fake testimonials or fake reviews.
- No guaranteed customers or revenue.
- No legal/tax/financial advice.
- No customer-facing campaigns without owner approval.

## CTA Strategy

Primary CTAs:

- Start setup.
- View pricing.
- Contact SONARA.

Secondary CTAs:

- View Business Builder.
- View Creator Studio.
- View Growth Studio.
- Read Security.

Do not push users into paid checkout until Stripe test/live mode, webhook handling, refund/dispute process, and owner payout documentation are verified.

## Owner Review Requirements

Owner confirmation is required before:

- Changing pricing.
- Publishing legal/policy text.
- Publishing proof/reviews.
- Sending customer-facing campaigns.
- Issuing refunds.
- Changing payout settings.
- Publishing public claims that imply compliance, security, results, or verification.

## Launch Offer Verdict

Status: `needs_review`.

The launch offer is clear enough for beta and owner review. Public paid launch should wait for Stripe verification, legal review, and final support/setup service operating procedures.
