# What the market is complaining about — 18 August 2026

Research behind the nine products in `lib/sonara-market-tools.cjs`. Every figure
is dated and sourced, because a number in marketing copy that nobody can check is
the thing this repository keeps removing.

**Review this before 18 February 2027.** These are survey figures from a moving
market; they age.

---

## Business Builder — the market's complaint is *inefficiency*, not price

- **52% of buyers switched business software because of inefficiency**, not price
  and not missing features — Software Advice, cited by
  [slbloggersupport](https://slbloggersupport.com/uncategorized/2026-guide-choosing-business-software-smb/), 2026.
- The feature that would actually save time is *"buried, missing, or available
  only on the enterprise tier that costs three times what you budgeted"* — same
  source.
- All-in-one platforms are *"built to serve the median business, not your
  business"*; moving everything onto one platform gains simplicity and loses
  specificity — same source.

**What that means for us.** Competing on breadth is competing where the
complaint is. The gap is small, specific, immediately-useful answers that a big
suite buries. Three of ours are deliberately narrow.

## Creator Studio — the biggest single number in the research

- **67% of creators reported contract-related disputes or payment issues in the
  past year** — HubSpot 2025 Creator Economy Report, cited by
  [InfluenceFlow](https://influenceflow.io/resources/contract-management-software-for-creators-the-complete-2026-guide/).
- **26% said payment delays from brands or platforms hurt their content
  production** — Visa 2025 global survey, same citation.
- Average creator income in the CreatorIQ pool was **$44,293**, with only **11%
  reporting six figures** —
  [CreatorIQ State of Creators 2026](https://www.creatoriq.com/press/releases/creatoriq-state-of-creators-report-2026).

**What that means for us.** Two thirds of the market has a paperwork problem, and
the average creator cannot afford a lawyer at $44k a year. The crowded answer is
contract *generators*. The gap is the moment before a contract exists — what was
agreed, when, and what happens when payment is late.

## Growth Studio — referrals are the top source and nobody measures them

- **83% of small businesses say customer referrals are their best acquisition
  source, up from 65% the year before; 87% for businesses with ten or fewer
  staff, up from 75%** —
  [LocaliQ Small Business Marketing Trends Report 2026](https://localiq.com/blog/small-business-marketing-trends-report-2026/).
- Local service businesses pay **$20–$200 per lead**; B2B pays **$420–$3,080** —
  [NewMedia](https://newmedia.com/blog/lead-generation-cost) and
  [Belkins](https://belkins.io/blog/b2b-cost-per-lead), 2026.
- Consumers now expect **higher star ratings *and recent* reviews**, and are
  looking beyond Google —
  [BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/).
- *"If inquiries rise but sales do not, the issue may be pricing, response time,
  fit, or follow-up"* — LocaliQ, same report.

**What that means for us.** The channel 83% of businesses call their best is the
one almost no software measures, because it has no ad pixel. And review tools
compete on rating while the survey says **recency** is what moved. Both are gaps
with a number behind them.

## Why these nine are defensible

| Product | The documented problem | Why it is not what competitors sell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price Rise Planner | Owners under-price and fear raising | Pricing tools compute a price. None answer *how many customers can I lose and still be better off* |
| Software Spend Auditor | Tool sprawl making businesses slower | The category's own products add to the stack; this one measures it |
| Quiet Month Cash Plan | Seasonal trough | Forecasting tools project a trend; this names the month you run out |
| Deal Memo Recorder | 67% dispute rate | Competitors generate contracts. This records what was agreed before one exists |
| Late Payment Escalation | 26% hurt by payment delays | Invoicing tools resend. This prices the delay and dates the escalation |
| Usage Rights Expiry | Licences lapse unnoticed | No mainstream creator tool tracks a licence *end* date |
| Referral Source Tracker | 83% call referrals their best source | Attribution tools need a click. Referrals have none |
| Review Recency Score | Recency now outweighs rating | Review tools optimise the average; this optimises the date |
| Enquiry Response Clock | Response time named as a cause | CRMs log the reply. This prices the wait |

## Profitability

All nine are deterministic: no model call, no provider, no network, **no
per-customer cost**. Gross margin on them is the hosting they share with every
other page. That is the point of building them this way rather than as prompts —
a tool with a per-use cost cannot sit on a free tier and act as the reason
somebody signs up.
