# Autopilot Limits

Autopilot exists to reduce routine work, not to replace owner judgment.

## Automatically Allowed

- Create internal tasks.
- Remind owner or admin.
- Draft messages.
- Update checklist status.
- Flag missing information.
- Retry safe webhook jobs.
- Summarize issues.
- Prepare recommendations.

## Not Automatically Allowed

- Send customer-facing messages.
- Publish offers.
- Change payment links.
- Change prices.
- Approve generated content.
- Issue refunds.
- Change legal or policy copy.
- Delete data.
- Change security settings.
- Publish proof, reviews, or trust claims.

## Always Blocked

- Change payout destination.
- Remove owner.
- Disable security gates.
- Delete audit logs.
- Send legal notices.
- Send deceptive claims.
- Publish fake reviews or proof.

## Why Full Autonomy Is Not Allowed

Money, legal, security, customer, public proof, and AI media decisions can create real harm. SONARA One can prepare the work, but the owner must confirm high-risk actions before execution.
