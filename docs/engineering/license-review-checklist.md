# License Review Checklist

- Permissive licenses such as MIT, BSD, and Apache-2.0 are generally reviewable with attribution obligations.
- GPL and AGPL require legal review before any production or SaaS use.
- Source-available and fair-code licenses require commercial-use review.
- Non-commercial licenses are blocked from revenue products unless the owner obtains separate rights.
- Model weights, datasets, media assets, and examples need separate license review.
- Do not ship derivative work until license compatibility is documented.
- Do not vendor external code until review is complete and attribution is tracked.
