# Market Pattern Lab

Market Pattern Lab captures public product and business patterns as research notes only.

## Scope

- Landing page layout.
- Pricing model.
- Onboarding flow.
- Dashboard UX.
- Admin controls.
- Paywall placement.
- Support flow.
- Trust/security copy.
- Agency offer packaging.
- CTA patterns.
- Mobile layout.
- Notification and sound UX.

## Rules

- Do not copy competitor code.
- Do not copy protected copy.
- Do not clone logos or brands.
- Do not bypass paywalls or private areas.
- Use only public observation, user-provided screenshots, and lawful research.
