# Growth Prompt Library

Growth Studio prompt templates help users draft campaign strategy, cold outreach, ad copy, referral campaigns, review responses, customer feedback analysis, SEO outlines, and growth planning.

Rules:

- Customer messaging requires permission context.
- Outreach, ads, referral campaigns, and review responses require owner approval before use.
- Fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake reviews, spam, and manipulative ranking are blocked.
- No output may be sent automatically.
