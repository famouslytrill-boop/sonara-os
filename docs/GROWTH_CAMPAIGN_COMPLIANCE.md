# Growth Campaign Compliance

Growth Studio campaign and outreach records are consent-first.

Implemented foundation:
- Campaign records.
- Campaign contacts.
- Campaign messages.
- Campaign events.
- Consent records.
- Opt-outs.
- Outreach approvals.
- Outreach audit logs.

Rules:
- SMS, calling, and voicemail campaigns cannot activate without consent.
- Consent source and timestamp must be recorded.
- Opt-out overrides all outreach.
- Risky campaigns require admin review.
- Ringless voicemail remains disabled by default.

Compliance warning:
"Phone, SMS, and voicemail outreach may be regulated. Confirm valid consent and honor opt-outs before contacting anyone."
