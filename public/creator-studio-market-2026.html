SONARA Creator Studio · Research direction · September 2026

One governed workspace from first idea to measurable release.

Creator Studio is being designed around the whole creator workflow: create, edit, organize, review, package, publish, monetize and measure—without locking the project itself to one model or provider.

Planning notice: this page describes product direction and research priorities. It does not claim that every capability shown here is currently available or connected to a customer account.
01 · Create

Multi-format creation

Video, image, music, voice, podcast, written and interactive work share one project context, rights boundary and generation history.

02 · Edit

Transcript + timeline

Move beyond generated files into editable transcripts, captions, scenes, timelines, versions and non-destructive project state.

03 · Organize

Creator Project Graph

Connect briefs, source assets, prompts, rights, versions, releases, channel packages, offers and analytics as one traceable body of work.

04 · Collaborate

Private review first

Team and client comments, approvals, revocable sharing, version review and optional live rooms before any public-social expansion.

05 · Publish

Package once, distribute deliberately

Prepare channel-specific video, short-form, audio, transcript, caption, thumbnail and metadata packages with human approval before publishing.

06 · Monetize

Commerce connected to the work

Use shared SONARA billing and commerce for releases, products, memberships and usage credits rather than building a separate payment rail.

07 · Measure

Evidence, not vanity dashboards

Keep source platform, period, timestamp, units and provenance attached to performance and revenue evidence.

08 · Assist

Agents with boundaries

Assist with research, packaging and repetitive production work while approvals, budgets, tenant scope, audit and deterministic workflow rules remain authoritative.

Target operating loop

Brief → Rights check → Create/import → Edit → Review → Approve → Package → Publish/export → Sell → Measure → Retrieve evidence → Plan the next version

Creator Studio owns

  • Creator projects and asset workflows
  • Media generation and editing experiences
  • Rights, provenance and release preparation
  • Private creative review and collaboration
  • Creator commerce and channel packaging
  • Creator-focused analytics and evidence

Shared or integrated

  • Identity, tenant security and authorization
  • Agents, durable workflows and approvals
  • Payments, usage credits and subscriptions
  • Database, storage, search/RAG and observability
  • External publishing and realtime media providers
  • Industry operations such as POS, fleet and trades
