SONARA Industries · Research Lab · 2026-09-22

Unified Commerce + Store Operations

The target is one commerce operating graph across owned web storefronts, physical stores, POS, kiosks, pickup, delivery and approved external sales channels—not separate databases for every surface.

Canonical commerce spine

Organization and location → product and variant → SKU/legitimate product identifiers → pricebook → cart/order → provider-confirmed payment state → inventory movement → fulfillment → receipt → return/refund → customer history → reconciliation.

Channel-specific interfaces may differ. Product, customer, order, payment-reference, inventory and audit truth must not.

Storefront + POS + kiosk

Owned storefronts should use server-authoritative price and availability. Staff POS should add scanner/search, role-gated discounts, tenders, returns, receipts and closeout. Kiosks should expose a constrained order surface with accessible touch interaction, device/session isolation and a staff-assistance path.

Inventory + procurement

Track on hand, allocated, safety stock, available to sell, confirmed inbound, movements, adjustments, cycle counts, transfers and supplier receipts by stock location. Replenishment recommendations should use deterministic demand, safety-stock, reorder-point, EOQ, sell-through, turnover, days-on-hand, GMROI, variance and weeks-of-supply calculations.

Own-brand product line

Model concept and specification, BOM/recipe, supplier or contract manufacturer, sample approval, purchasing/production, QA, landed cost, packaging/label revision, legitimate identifiers, inventory receipt, launch assortment, sell-through, replenishment, markdown and retirement.

Specialist CAD, PLM, laboratory, certification and regulated manufacturing systems remain adapters where appropriate.

Brick-and-mortar

A physical store requires operating controls beyond a register: staff roles, receiving, stock locations, cycle counts, opening/closing cash, device health, payment-provider readiness, pickup procedures, returns, accessibility, network resilience, exception review and daily reconciliation.

Omnichannel proof

Required proof includes concurrency-safe stock reservations, buy-online-pickup-in-store, ship-from-store, buy-in-store-ship-to-customer, cross-channel returns, offline/degraded reconciliation, provider-confirmed online and in-person payment states, multi-location reconciliation and one-location canary evidence.

Agent boundary

Models may draft product copy, summarize reviews, explain anomalies, recommend merchandising and propose replenishment. Deterministic policy retains price arithmetic, tax, stock mutations, payment/refund state, order settlement, purchasing authority, customer consent, cash reconciliation and destructive changes.

2026 benchmark pattern

Shopify, Square, Toast, Odoo, Lightspeed, Stripe, Medusa and Saleor all reinforce different parts of the same pattern: unified product and inventory state, multiple sales surfaces, fulfillment and return workflows, and provider-backed payment infrastructure. GS1 and merchant-feed standards reinforce clean product identity.

These products and repositories are research inputs. Their feature counts, hardware catalogs and extension catalogs are not SONARA-native capabilities.

Build sequence

  1. Canonical product, variant, channel, location, order and inventory contracts.
  2. Deterministic commerce metrics and reconciliation.
  3. Owned storefront and one provider-backed online checkout.
  4. Returns/refunds and fulfillment evidence.
  5. One physical-location POS canary.
  6. BOPIS and cross-channel returns.
  7. Multi-location transfers, ship-from-store and replenishment.
  8. Own-brand sourcing/manufacturing depth.
  9. Approved product feeds and marketplace adapters.
  10. Scale only after measured reliability, adoption and cost evidence.
