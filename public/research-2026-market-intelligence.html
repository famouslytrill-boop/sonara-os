SONARA Industries · Research Lab · 2026-09-20

Market + Technology Intelligence

External evidence is translated into product architecture without granting providers, repositories, models, agents, payments, or workers production authority.

Core market signal

AI adoption is broad, but governed agent execution is still less mature. SONARA's defensible layer is the operating system around models: tenant context, deterministic policy, workflows, approvals, evidence, evaluation, cost controls, observability, and rollback.

System of record + intelligence + action

Restaurant, field-service, fleet, retail, creator, manufacturing, property, and service workflows should share canonical records and state machines. AI should reason over current business data and propose or perform only explicitly authorized actions.

Agent-ready commerce

Catalogs, availability, checkout, subscriptions, usage metering, delegated spend, refunds, entitlements, and reconciliation should be machine-readable. Payment credentials and final authority stay outside model context.

Reusable vertical packs

Restaurant/POS/kiosk, skilled trades and field service, fleet/trucking/delivery, retail/ecommerce, creator/social/media, manufacturing/robotics, real estate/rentals, education/translation, and interactive 3D experiences should compose one shared identity, workflow, commerce, communications, analytics, and evidence fabric.

Build vs. integrate

SONARA owns workflow, policy, tenant boundaries, data context, UX, evidence, formulas, and vertical composition. Payment rails, telecom, app-store billing, model hosting, specialist accounting/payroll/insurance, POS hardware, telematics, robotics, CAD execution, distribution APIs, cloud, and GPU infrastructure remain governed integrations.

Deterministic portfolio formula

0.25*pain + 0.25*platform_reuse + 0.20*data_advantage + 0.15*monetization + 0.15*adoption_readiness - 0.12*integration_risk - 0.13*regulatory_risk

This is an internal prioritization heuristic, not a revenue forecast, market prediction, investment score, or promise of customer demand.

Engineering sequence

  1. Identity, tenant, entitlement and approval contracts.
  2. Versioned workflows, action runs, idempotency and durable evidence.
  3. Bounded agents, grounded RAG, evaluation and cost controls.
  4. Unified customer conversation and service timeline.
  5. Canonical catalog, order, payment, refund and subscription state.
  6. Restaurant, field-service and fleet composition.
  7. Creator/social/media distribution and rights-aware commerce.
  8. Offline field/device inputs, passkeys and security monitoring.
  9. Forecasting, optimization and operations formulas.
  10. External/MCP adapters, spatial experiences and regulated integrations only after proof gates.
