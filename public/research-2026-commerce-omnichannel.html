SONARA Industries · Research Lab · 2026-09-22

Commerce + Omnichannel Intelligence

One canonical commerce graph for owned storefronts, physical stores, POS, kiosks, B2B, fulfillment and reviewed agentic-commerce adapters.

Core decision

Do not create separate order, customer, product or inventory truth for every channel. Business Builder should own a shared commerce lifecycle, with SONARA Nexus providing identity, policy, workflow, payments, evidence and adapter controls.

What customers should be able to run

Owned ecommerce storefronts, brick-and-mortar stores, staff POS, self-service kiosks, QR order/pay, pickup, local delivery, shipping, B2B ordering, returns, supplier purchasing and eventually approved marketplace and agentic channels.

Canonical flow

catalog → availability → cart → checkout → order → payment state → fulfillment → return/exchange/refund → reconciliation → analytics

The same product, inventory and order records must survive the entire journey across web and physical channels.

Physical-store depth

POS and kiosk need barcode/PLU support, location inventory, receipt evidence, offline/degraded states, accessibility and deterministic tender handoff. Kiosk remains a constrained channel over the same commerce state rather than a separate application database.

Inventory + supply

Track explicit stock movements, reservations, safety stock, transfers, cycle counts, suppliers, purchase orders, partial receiving, unit/landed cost and aging. AI may recommend replenishment; configured policy controls commitment.

available_to_promise = max(0, on_hand - reserved - safety_stock - blocked)

Returns are core commerce

Returns, exchanges, inspection/disposition, refund state and fraud evidence belong in the canonical order lifecycle. They should not be handled as unstructured support notes.

Agentic commerce

SONARA should remain protocol-neutral. UCP, ACP, MCP commerce tools and payment-network agent standards are adapter surfaces. Agents may search, compare and draft; deterministic commands retain authority over inventory, orders, refunds, pricing, publication and spend.

Own-line / private-label products

Support product concept, supplier/manufacturer, samples, specifications, production batch, receiving/QA, identifiers/packaging, allocation, selling, returns, demand evidence and next-production decisions while leaving specialist CAD, regulated manufacturing and safety-critical control to reviewed systems.

2026 market references

Research includes the U.S. Census Bureau, NRF, Shopify, Square, Toast, Lightspeed, GS1, Google UCP, OpenAI ACP, Visa, Mastercard, Stripe and Amazon Buy with Prime. These are market references, not claims of installed integrations.
