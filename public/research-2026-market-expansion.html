Build depth around a proven customer workflow. Show what works, where a human stays in control, and what evidence supports the claim.

Research finding

Small businesses are adopting more technology, creators are drawing larger advertising investment, restaurants depend on smooth off-premises service, and contractors already use several disconnected operational tools. These are useful demand signals; none proves demand for SONARA by itself.

The best next move is a narrow operating loop with real users, measurable completion, and a real provider boundary. Another long list of industries or integrations would add surface area without proving adoption.

One message across three studios

SONARA One helps small teams move work from first contact to completed service and repeat business, with one connected workspace and clear human control.

  • Business Builder: organize customers, services, bookings or jobs, records, payment status, and follow-up.
  • Creator Studio: organize creative work, rights, assets, publishing, and monetization records.
  • Growth Studio: manage campaigns, leads, offers, referrals, and measured follow-up.

Public claims should name supported workflows and verified providers. Do not promise universal coverage, full autonomy, or replacement of specialist systems without proof.

First customer loop to validate

inquiry → customer record → quote/booking → scheduled work → completion evidence → invoice/payment status → follow-up

Test this with a small service-business pilot. Identify each live, manual, and unavailable step. Track time to first real record, first completed workflow, repeat usage, provider failures, and support effort.

Keep the product useful without AI

Permissions, prices, workflow state, payment status, schedules, retries, and audit records belong in deterministic, testable code. AI can assist with drafts, summaries, and recommendations; human approval and server-side rules remain responsible for sensitive actions.

Media creation needs explicit provenance, rights and consent, pinned export settings, progress, cancellation, and a fallback when a provider, worker, device, or network is unavailable.

Make every action useful

Each visible control should have one canonical route or mutation, a permission, loading and result states, an accessible name, and a recovery path. For a new workspace, offer one guided first action and clearly labeled sample records where useful. Do not create fake customer records simply to hide a zero count.

Evidence snapshot

  • The U.S. Chamber's 2025 technology report says nearly 60% of small businesses report using AI in operations; the report describes survey responses, not purchase intent for SONARA.
  • NFIB's 2025 survey reports 19% of surveyed small businesses with a website accept payments there; treat this as a directional survey signal.
  • The National Restaurant Association's 2025 report identifies service speed, customer service, intuitive ordering/payment, offers, and loyalty as off-premises repeat-business essentials.
  • IAB projected U.S. creator advertising spend at $37B in 2025, up 26% year over year; ad spend is not creator income or SONARA's addressable market.
  • ServiceTitan reports that its 2025 survey of 1,000+ commercial specialty contractors found 80% using communication tools, 51% project management software, and 40% workforce-management software.

For key routes, measure real-user Core Web Vitals at the 75th percentile. Google's good thresholds are LCP ≤2.5s, INP ≤200ms, and CLS ≤0.1.

Next proof gates

  1. Bring the work branch current and review open PRs and exact-head checks.
  2. Prove one authorized, read-only connector for one tenant, including revocation, deletion, and recovery.
  3. Run a five-user service-business pilot and measure completion, repeat usage, and support needs.
  4. Gate release on tenant isolation, route/action coverage, accessibility, real-user speed, backup recovery, and security evidence.
  5. Expand to another vertical only after a separate pilot and verified provider proof.

Production deployment, provider spend, external campaigns, and live tenant activation remain separate controlled actions.
